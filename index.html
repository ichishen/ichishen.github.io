My research investigates market manipulation, investor trust, and misconduct and scams in financial markets. I am interested in exposing how perpetrators commit fraudulent behaviors so that regulators can enact safeguards to protect the general public.

Update July 2025: I am recruiting PhD students to work on exciting projects using AI and machine learning to study China capital markets.
